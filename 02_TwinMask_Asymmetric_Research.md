# Proposal 2 — TwinMask: Asymmetric Conditional Graph Inpainting
## Dependency-aware masked diffusion for incomplete text-attributed graphs
Research and implementation specification | 24 November 2026 | Proposed, not validated

### 1. Problem and scientific claim
A text-attributed graph can contain partially missing text and unknown links. Existing semantic/structural diffusion models already jointly process the modalities. The proposed scientific question is narrower: can one learn directional predictive utility of observed text and topology and spend a fixed reconstruction budget on the modality whose completion is expected to improve subsequent joint reconstruction? Target actual conditional imputation of text tokens and candidate edge states, not merely latent alignment or node classification. Explicitly separate unknown links from confirmed negative pairs. The novelty candidate is calibrated, directional value-of-information scheduling with corrective remasking under asymmetric missingness; independent timesteps, uncertainty gates, and remasking individually are established ideas.

Prior internal evidence from the TwinMask discussion: joint training showed promising robustness relative to single-task training, but tied schedules sometimes win; one-step text NLL outperformed reported multi-step paths (Cora 4.243 vs best eight-step 4.547; PubMed 2.966 vs 3.117). Treat these as reported preliminary observations, not independently reproduced measurements. Before running, reproduce original checkpoints, masks, negative sampling, and metric definitions. Do not claim multi-step generation works until a comparable free-running evaluation establishes it. DIVERGE bridge results are from embedding-level denoisers and do not validate this token-level architecture.

### 3. Formal task and leakage-safe observations
Let V be known nodes; X_i token sequence, P candidate unordered pairs (or ordered pairs for directed graph), A_e in {0,1} observed ground truth for evaluation. Masks M_X and M_A indicate unknown entries. Edge states are PRESENT, ABSENT, UNKNOWN. In real incomplete graphs, unobserved pairs need not be true negatives; benchmark negative sampling assumes known reference graph and must disclose open-world limitations. Use sparse observed adjacency for messages, separate candidate pair table for prediction. Do not treat all O(N²) pairs as known negatives or enumerate them. Candidate selection must use observable inputs; do not expose whether a candidate came from positive or negative sampling to the network. Report conditional ranking within candidate set separately from retrieval recall over all hidden links. Distinguish known absent pair from merely unobserved pair.

Link splits first: split undirected pairs before duplicating directions, remove validation/test positives from all training graph, sampling, degree features, positional encodings and caches. For each evaluation case reveal only allowed context. For text imputation split observed and withheld token/node content; clean target text must not enter embeddings or node IDs used as an indirect lookup. In transductive experiments original non-hidden text and observed edges are allowed, no labels for pretraining. Inductive experiments reserve nodes entirely. Evaluate random edge missingness, degree-dependent missingness and missing bridges separately; random token, span and whole-node text missingness. Fit mask distributions on train/development only.

### 4. Discrete forward process and model
For modality m in {X,A}, absorbing mask process q(z_t|z_0) = (1-m_m(t)) delta(z_0) + m_m(t) delta(MASK), monotonic m_m(t), m_m(0)=0, m_m(1)=1. For training sample t_X,t_A independently; mixed distribution also includes tied states and conditional corners (clean text/noisy edges and vice versa). Example mixture .5 independent, .25 tied, .25 corners; validate rather than claim optimality. For natural missingness, unavailable ground truth is excluded from loss, not treated as negative. Generate masks consistently across undirected pair directions. Never mask special padding tokens. Distinguish immutable observations from generated mutable tokens/edges.

Architecture: DistilRoBERTa token encoder (initial max length 128, 256 sensitivity), pooled node representations, timestep embeddings for both clocks, graph mixer with 2–3 sparse observed-edge message-passing layers, hidden dimension 256. Inject neighborhood context into token states via cross-attention or FiLM conditioning. Pair decoder takes symmetric [h_i+h_j, |h_i-h_j|, h_i*h_j, time embedding, edge state] for undirected graphs; for directed use ordered embeddings. Token head predicts vocabulary logits [B,L,Vocab]; edge head two logits per candidate [P,2]. Unknown edges are not ordinary message-passing edges; optional predicted soft edges are a separate ablation. Avoid dense full graph token attention. For masked nodes use learned mask embedding, not cached clean embedding. Optional language encoder freezing or LoRA specified separately, same trainable parameters in compared variants.

Loss: L = lambda_X * sum_CE(masked supervised tokens)/n_X + lambda_A * sum_CE(masked labeled candidates)/n_A. Empty mask branch contributes zero; track denominators. This is a masked reconstruction surrogate, not automatically exact NELBO. For formal diffusion likelihood use the time-dependent weights from an explicitly derived reverse kernel and disclose any reweighting. Class-balanced candidate loss may distort posterior probability; calibrate on validation with natural candidate priors or report sampled-prior calibration. Use lambda_X=lambda_A=1 initially, track gradient norms, tune only on validation. AdamW: encoder lr 2e-5 if unfrozen, graph/heads 1e-3; wd .01; 100 epochs with early stop patience 10. Use fp16/bf16, gradient accumulation, central node losses only. All values starting points.

### 5. Directional predictive utility (main novelty hypothesis)
Define state s with visible text and visible edge evidence. For a masked text unit, compare target held-out negative log likelihood with and without access to observed structural evidence using the SAME denoiser trained with condition dropout. g_A→X = loss_X(without A) - loss_X(with A). Analogously g_X→A. Differences in cross-entropies are predictive utility estimates, not proven mutual information; model mismatch and mask distribution affect them. Paired mask and targets isolate evidence contribution. Compare with shuffled topology, degree-preserving rewiring, randomized text and matched removed-neighbor counts. Compute gains per node, corruption bin and degree class; estimate global and local asymmetry. No hidden labels at inference. To predict realized utility, train a small regressor u_phi(s) on labeled *reconstruction targets in training graphs only*, inputs entropy, degree, observed fraction, semantic agreement computed exclusively on visible tokens, candidate edge uncertainty, previous-step changes. Split training rollouts and validation ones. Do not use actual withheld target loss at inference; oracle controller only upper bound. Calibrate estimates by predicted-versus-realized gain bins, negative gain accuracy and Spearman correlation. Compare entropy-only feature predictor and constant mean.

Important distinction: current directional context gain is not identical to future gain from revealing generated predictions. Predicting a correct token may help edge prediction but a hallucinated token may harm it. Therefore controller targets must use free-running model predictions, not teacher-forced true reveals, when learning action values.

### 6. Reverse process and scheduling
V0: reproduce one-shot fill, diagonal, text-first and edge-first. For scheduled decreasing mask rate m(t), among currently masked eligible units reveal fraction (m(t)-m(t'))/m(t), using sampled token/pair posterior. Clamp all observed units, and match total denoiser calls B. Confidence-ranked unmasking is heuristic unless corresponding probabilistic kernel is specified. V1: corrective remasking of low-confidence mutable generated units, never observations. Compare standard ReMDM-inspired sampler where tractable; heuristic version uses budget <=10% mutable entries per step and logs effective reveals. Freeze stochastic seed and mask for paired comparisons. V2: adaptive controller selects one action a in {text block, edge block, joint block, remask+refine, stop}. Choose same block fraction or explicitly account for action compute cost. Action training: for each simulated free-running training state, roll one candidate action with matched random draws, recompute true target losses on immutable withheld training targets, optionally short lookahead. Target R(s,a) = [L_eval(s)-L_eval(s_after)]/cost(a); L_eval uses the same fixed withheld set at each state, not only remaining masks. For generated units score predicted distributions with self-evidence masked again or use a held-out scoring subset to avoid trivial self-copy; explicitly define this scoring rule and ablate it. Train regression head for R and use argmax expected gain per measured cost at test. Include a terminal completion constraint: reserve enough remaining budget to fill required entries; stopping early cannot leave unscored missing items. Compare random, diagonal, fixed modality-first, confidence-only, current entropy, predicted directional utility, and oracle with true training-like target access (analysis only). No RL needed until benefit from lightweight policy demonstrated.

Pseudo algorithm:
```
s = observed_tokens_and_pairs_with_unknown_masks()
for step in range(B):
    logits = denoiser(s, tx, ta)
    features = observable_state_and_confidence(s, logits)
    a = choose_action(features, remaining_budget)
    s = reveal_or_remask_mutable_units(s, logits, a)
    assert observed_entries_unchanged(s)
return completed_state_and_probabilities(s)
```
Log every action, cost, reveals, errors against withheld labels offline, and frozen observation checks. Count any extra forward passes needed by controller. Compare at equal calls AND equal wall time. Do not evaluate one-shot by teacher forcing and iterative by generated text under different likelihoods: report comparable conditional masked CE, and final free-running exact/token/semantic metrics separately.

### 8. Evaluation design
Primary endpoint: conditional imputation across corruption severities, not merely downstream accuracy. Cora/CiteSeer/PubMed pilot, WikiCS/ogbn-arxiv plus verified noncitation TAG with raw text for generalization. Start with raw text provenance; PubMed bag-of-words alone is insufficient for token reconstruction. Split fixed across methods; 3 seeds for pilot, >=10 paired final seeds where feasible. Severity text/edge mask rates {0,.25,.5,.75,.9}; all-missing both is nonidentifiable without additional node conditions, label separately and do not promise exact recovery. Include whole-node text loss, hub-targeted edges, noisy graph (false edges) distinct from missingness. Synthetic SBM with controllable class homophily, lexical signal strength, edge noise, degree and feature/structure alignment; vary factors independently, avoid using true class labels at inference. Test whether directional gain tracks designed signal, not simply homophily.

Baselines: token MLM with no graph; graph-conditioned one-shot MLM; GAE/VGAE link prediction; text similarity link prediction; graph structural heuristics common neighbors/Adamic-Adar; Feature Propagation and GraphMAE/2 for embedding completion only; tied and independent clocks with equal capacity; separate text/edge denoisers; UDPD and TAG-DLM at overlapping endpoints if code available; fixed schedule with ReMDM-style correction; confidence-only controller. Report incompatible tasks honestly, no made-up results. Compare identical candidates and observed contexts for all link baselines. Optional raw token semantic scoring using frozen evaluation encoder not trained on test targets. For downstream GraphSAGE use same training labels and graph inputs, no hidden true target at test.

Metrics: conditional token CE on fixed masked targets, token accuracy, exact span recovery, semantic similarity, edge AUROC/AP, Hits@K/MRR with explicit negatives, candidate retrieval recall, Brier/ECE, accuracy/macro-F1 downstream, robustness curve AUC normalized by corruption interval, GPUh, peak VRAM, inference latency, calls. Report both directions separately and joint quality with prespecified normalization; no arbitrary average of NLL and AUC. Use paired run-level CIs, seed and split variance, Holm correction for secondary hypotheses. Gain regression evaluated on unseen corruption patterns and graphs. Primary contrasts: adaptive vs best validation-selected fixed policy, corrected vs uncorrected sampler, independent vs tied schedule. Multi-step gains must be assessed free-running and at equal budgets.

### 9. Stage gates and journal positioning
Stage A (reproduction): reconcile prior one-step advantage; compare identical masks and metrics. If additional steps only worsen output, test remasking/self-conditioning and model-generated rollouts before controller. Stage B (scientific evidence): establish directional gain varies with observable graph/text conditions and is predictable on held-out cases, including negative gain and synthetic intervention controls. Stage C (new method): adaptive scheduling improves at least one primary reconstruction endpoint without material degradation in other, or yields same quality with fewer model calls, against best fixed and confidence-only controls; preregister margins based on pilot variance, not final test outcomes. Stage D: demonstrate generalization on several datasets and missingness types, plus compute and leakage audit. If one-shot remains best at all budgets, pivot to directional dependency analysis and one-shot adaptive conditioning only if clearly distinct from UDPD; otherwise stop. No fabricated theoretical guarantee or Q1 probability.

Potential title: When Text and Topology Disagree: Directional Utility and Budgeted Masked Diffusion for Incomplete Text-Attributed Graphs. KBS, IPM, ESWA and Neural Networks are scope candidates; verify current quartile/category. Main paper must center on imputation and conditional evidence, not ensemble diversity (MADE/DIVERGE). A strong result needs more than adding a gate to UDPD. Report limitations: open-world negatives, imperfect semantic ground truth, model calibration, high computational cost, and nonidentifiability under severe missingness.
